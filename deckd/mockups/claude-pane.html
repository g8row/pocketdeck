Conversation layout, state by state

Option A shows your last prompt and Claude's last reply in large type, with one side card for whatever the session needs right now. Below are the ten states it has to handle. In each one, scroll the reply with a mouse wheel or trackpad: it is a real scroll area.

The side card is the stateBlue while working, amber when it needs you, green when done. The reply on the left stays put, so you keep reading while you decide.
Choices take over the left columnA question or a plan replaces the reply, because answering is the only thing to do. "Show reply" gets the reply back.
Text scrolls, never truncatesLong replies scroll by touch, with fades at the clipped edges, a thin position bar, and a "more below" pill until you reach the end.
